## Decay
Certain conditions with a numerical value but without an explicit duration (such as *Inspiration*) can be marked as *decaying*. A decaying condition reduces its value by one at the start of each of the affected creature's turns, until it reaches zero at which point the creature loses the condition.
# Positive Status Effects
- **Sanctuary**: reduce damage taken (except true damage)
- **Inspiration**: increases primary attributes
- **Status Resistance**: resist neg status effects
# Negative Status Effects
- **Stun**: lose X turns
- **Paralyze**: lose X actions
- **Bind**: cannot move or use [movement] actions
- **Slow**: MOV reduction
- **Fear**: cant act and is forced to move away from source
- **Silence**: cant use skills except basic attacks
