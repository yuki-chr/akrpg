Morale is a shared [[Arknights The Roleplaying Game#Reserves|reserve]] used by the players. It does not have a maximum capacity, but it is emptied at the end of each encounter and cannot be accumulated or spent outside of combat.
# Gaining Morale
Morale can be gained in the following ways:
- **At the start of each combat round** the party gains 1d morale.
- [[Classes#Vanguard|Vanguard]] characters can generate morale with their skills, traits and talents.
- Certain enemies or objectives might grant additional morale during combat, as determined by the GM.
# Spending Morale
Characters can spend morale in a variety of ways, but each character can only spend morale **once per round** at most.
## Generic Uses
The following is a list of ways of spending morale that are available to all characters:
- **Charge**: Before starting your movement during your turn, you can declare a Charge. If you do, spend 1d morale and [[Arknights The Roleplaying Game#Flat Rolls|flat roll]] it. Depending on the result, you gain MOV for this turn as follows:
	- **1-2**: MOV +1
	- **3-4**: MOV +2
	- **5-6**: MOV +3
## Morale Boost
Certain skills allow spending morale to gain additional effects. This will be notated in the skill description as `boost X` where X is the morale cost of boosting the action. The effects of boosting the skill will be explained by the description and can range from simply increasing its numbers to giving it additional effects.
